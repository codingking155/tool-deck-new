Sheaf privacy policy

For the Sheaf website and the Sheaf Android app. Last updated 8 October 2026.

The short version

Sheaf works entirely on your device. The PDFs, photos and camera images you open in Sheaf are never uploaded, and we collect no personal data.

Your files

Merging, splitting, compressing, converting, signing, password protection and every other tool runs inside the app (or your browser tab). Files are read from your device and the results are saved back to it: in the Android app, to Documents/Sheaf. Nothing is sent to a server.

Files you share to Sheaf from another app are copied into the app's private cache so Sheaf can read them, and are deleted within about an hour.

Camera

The Android app asks for camera access only to scan documents and read QR codes. Camera images are processed on the phone and are not stored or sent anywhere. Only pages you choose to keep become part of a PDF you save. You can turn camera access off at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of Sheaf keeps working.

Storage

On Android 9 and older, the app asks for storage access so it can save files into your Documents folder. Newer Android versions need no permission for this.

Stored on your device

  • Your chosen scan look (Colour, Greyscale, Document or Original).
  • Your last 30 QR scans, so you can find them again. Clear them any time with Clear under Recent scans.
  • The details you last typed into the QR code maker (such as your UPI ID), so your code is ready next time. Wi-Fi passwords are not kept.

Uninstalling the app removes both.

No tracking

The Sheaf app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and does not share data with third parties.

Links in QR codes

When you choose to open a link, a UPI payment or a phone number read from a QR code, Sheaf hands it to the app that handles it, such as your browser or UPI app. That app's own privacy policy applies from then on.

Contact

Questions about this policy: write to the developer email shown on Sheaf’s Google Play listing.
